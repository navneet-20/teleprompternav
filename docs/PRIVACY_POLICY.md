# Teleprompter Pro Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

Teleprompter Pro is a local teleprompter utility developed by Navneet Kumar.

## Information we collect
The current version does not collect, sell, or share names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, location, photos, videos, audio recordings, advertising identifiers, account credentials, payment information, or analytics profiles.

Scripts entered into the app are processed locally for display, word counting, timing and scrolling. The current version does not upload script content to a developer-controlled server.

## Permissions
**Display over other apps:** used for the floating teleprompter overlay above another application.

**Notifications:** used for the Android foreground-service notification while the floating teleprompter is active.

**Foreground service:** used only after the user starts the floating teleprompter and only while that feature is active.

## Storage
Current script and settings processing is local to the device. There is no developer-controlled cloud account or database.

## Third parties
The current app does not integrate advertising, analytics, social-login, or cloud-storage SDKs.

Google Play and Android system services may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

## Children's privacy
The app is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically directed to children.

## Deletion
Because there is no developer-controlled account or cloud profile, there is no remote account to delete. Users can clear app data through Android settings or uninstall the app.

## Changes
If data practices change, this policy will be updated before the relevant release.

## Contact
Privacy questions should be sent to the contact email published on the Google Play listing.

This policy must always match the behavior of the submitted binary.
